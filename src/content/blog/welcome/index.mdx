## Not only a developer blog

I have spent eight years building software, most of it on the web, currently
leading frontend for a suite of crypto applications with a great team. So yes —
a fair share of what lands here will be technical.

But I did not want a blog that only holds engineering. The writing I keep
coming back to from other people is rarely the tutorial. It is somebody
thinking in public: about how teams actually work, about the gap between the
architecture diagram and the thing running in production, about what it costs
to be the person who says "no, let's do it properly."

So this is a personal space. Ideas, opinions, the occasional argument with my
past self. Some posts will have code. Some will not.

## What to expect

A few things I already know I want to write about:

- **Decisions, not tutorials.** There are enough posts explaining what a tool
  does. Fewer explain why someone picked it over the alternative, and what
  that choice cost six months later.
- **The parts nobody documents.** Technical leadership, mentoring, the work of
  turning a vague business ask into something an engineer can actually build.
- **Things I got wrong.** These tend to be the most useful ones to read, and
  the least comfortable to write.

## On frequency

I am not going to promise a schedule I will not keep. I have a few drafts
already in progress and more articles coming in the next days. They will
arrive when they are worth reading rather than when a calendar says so.

If any of that sounds interesting, the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) is the honest way
to follow along — no newsletter, no signup, no tracking.

Thanks for being early.
